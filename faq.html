Frequently Asked Questions

Is BarcodePro free?

Yes — every tool, including bulk export and the scanner, is free with no usage limits.

Do you store my barcodes or scanned images?

No. Generation and decoding both happen locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.

Can I use generated barcodes commercially?

Yes, codes you generate are yours to use for any purpose, commercial included.

Which barcode types are supported?

CODE128, CODE39, EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E, ITF-14, MSI, Pharmacode, QR Code, Data Matrix, PDF417, Aztec, GS1 and Codabar.

Can I download PNG and SVG?

Yes — barcodes and QR codes can both be exported as PNG or SVG depending on the tool.

How does the Bulk Generator work?

Upload a CSV (or paste a list) of values, pick a code type, and BarcodePro generates every code and packages them into a single downloadable ZIP.

What image formats does the Scanner accept?

JPG, PNG and WEBP. Clear, well-lit, straight-on photos decode most reliably.
