Matching real label sheets

The rows, columns and dimensions here are deliberately manual rather than tied to a specific brand of label paper, since sheet layouts vary by region and supplier. Measure your actual label sheet (or check its product page) for the exact label width, height and gap, and set the page size to match what your printer expects — most home and office printers default to US Letter in North America and A4 elsewhere.

Getting a clean print

Before running a full batch of labels, print a single test page and check it against your physical sheet without loading real labels into the printer — many printers let you preview or print to PDF first. If the grid is slightly offset, adjust your printer's scaling to "100% / Actual size" rather than "Fit to page," since scaling changes the physical label size and can misalign everything after the first row.

Where the values come from

Paste a plain list, upload a CSV with a value column, or drop in a file exported from the serial generator. For very large runs, the bulk generator is still the better choice for producing individual PNG files — this tool is specifically for arranging codes onto a physical sheet you intend to print and cut or peel apart.
